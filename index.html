Warm greetings of peace and good day!

We, the undersigned 3rd-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) students of Arellano University – Jose Abad Santos Campus (Pasay City), are currently conducting our Human-Computer Interaction 2 (HCI 2) research entitled:

RESEARCH TITLE
“Interactive Design and Usability Testing of AI-Enhanced Wearable Health Dashboards for Gen Z”

This research investigation aims to evaluate the usability, ergonomics, predictive explainability (XAI), and interface design efficacy of a web-based artificial intelligence health telemetry dashboard tailored to the behavioral preferences and physiological habits of the Generation Z cohort (ages 12 to 27).

In connection with this, we are conducting a structured usability evaluation protocol and standardized survey utilizing the System Usability Scale (SUS) and qualitative biofeedback measures. Your candid feedback, objective ratings, and experiential insights as a Gen Z participant will be of paramount significance in determining the success and practical validity of this study.

Please be assured that all information, demographic data, and responses provided in this evaluation will be handled with utmost confidentiality and strict compliance with the provisions of Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012). Your participation is entirely voluntary, and your responses will be used solely for academic research and interface optimization.

Thank you very much for your valuable time, cooperation, and meaningful contribution to this scholarly pursuit.
