Dear Valued Gen Z Participant / Respondent,

Warm greetings of peace and good day!

We, the undersigned 3rd-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) students of Arellano University – Jose Abad Santos Campus (Pasay City), are currently conducting our Human-Computer Interaction 2 (HCI 2) research entitled:

“Interactive Design and Usability Testing of AI-Enhanced Wearable Health Dashboards for Gen Z”

This study tests how easy, comfy, and clear our web health dashboard is for Gen Z (ages 12 to 27).

With this, you will try our dashboard and answer a short survey using the System Usability Scale (SUS). Your honest answers as a Gen Z student will help decide if this study works.

Your info and answers will stay private and follow the rules of Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012). Joining is voluntary, and your answers will be used only for school research.

Thank you for your time and help with our school study.
